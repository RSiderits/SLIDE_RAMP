STL File Summary – Microscope Slide Label Removal Holder

File name: Label_Remover.stl

The Microscope Slide Label Removal Holder is a 3D-printable 
laboratory engineering control designed to safely stabilize a 
standard 25 × 75 mm glass microscope slide during removal of 
adhesive-backed slide labels. The device was developed by the 
DLS Safety Committee under the leadership of Richard Siderits MD, 
to reduce the risks associated with simultaneous handling of 
two recognized “sharps”: a razor blade and a glass microscope slide.

The holder supports the slide at an optimized 30° working angle, 
incorporates guide rails to prevent lateral slide movement, 
and includes a bench-edge stop to improve stability during scraping. 
The design helps reduce the risk of:

Razor blade lacerations
Glass breakage and associated sharps injuries
Exposure to biological residue
Specimen damage during relabeling
Ergonomic strain during repetitive use

The device prints without support material, uses approximately 18 grams 
of filament, and was validated using PLA on a Bambu Lab X2D FDM printer 
at 15% hex infill, with a typical print time of approximately 32 minutes. 
The STL is freely available for non-commercial laboratory safety applications.

Before Printing
Inspect the STL in your slicer for correct orientation.
Confirm dimensions fit a standard microscope slide.
Verify that no support structures are automatically added.
After Printing
Allow the print to cool completely before removal.
Inspect for:
Cracks
Sharp edges
Warping
Incomplete guide rails
Remove any minor burrs before laboratory use.
Test fit with a non-critical blank slide before operational deployment.
Recommended Materials
PLA – easiest, low warping, suitable for bench use
PETG – greater chemical resistance
TPU – optional for specialized grip modifications

For laboratory implementation, printed devices should be visually 
inspected before use and incorporated into an SOP-based engineering 
control program.
